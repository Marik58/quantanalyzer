# Evidence for every agent

## Base rates: S&P 500 members, 12 months ahead

From 2011-01-31 to 2026-08-31, a usable S&P 500
member beat SPY over the next 12 months 44.7% of the time and returned
25% or more 29.6% of the time (n = 66,487 stock-months; the
windows overlap). A forecaster that always says these numbers is the Base-Rate
Forecaster (charters §13). Beating it is the minimum.
