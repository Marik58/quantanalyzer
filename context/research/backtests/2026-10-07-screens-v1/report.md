## Known limits (read before quoting any number above)

- **Survivorship:** member-months without prices are mostly companies that were bought out or failed. They are left out, not guessed, and the table above counts them. Their effect on the result could go either way (buyouts usually pay a premium; failures lose).
- **Large caps only, 2011 onward:** machine-readable SEC filings start around 2010, and the universe is the S&P 500. Small and mid caps, where growth and value effects are said to be strongest, aren't tested.
- **Yahoo's adjusted prices** are imperfect around spinoffs.
- **Banks and insurers** often lack operating income in their filings, so they rarely pass either screen (they stay in the pool).
- **One trial each.** Every rule change is a new trial and is recorded; with many trials, a t of 2 is expected by luck, which is why the bar is 3.
